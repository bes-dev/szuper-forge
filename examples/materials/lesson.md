# Everyday English: at a café

tea — чай
coffee — кофе
milk — молоко

Use “please” to make a request polite.
“Tea, please.” means “Чай, пожалуйста.”
“With milk?” means “С молоком?”

Dialogue situation: a customer orders tea and the server asks about milk.
The customer wants tea, not coffee. Use these facts for a short listening scene.
